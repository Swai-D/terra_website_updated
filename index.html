Who we help

Lenders, investors and regulators increasingly ask Tanzanian companies for environmental and ESG information. We help you answer clearly and with evidence.

  • Based in Tegeta, Dar es Salaam
  • Reporting aligned to GRI and IFRS S1 and S2
  • IFC Performance Standards where lenders require them

A simple, documented process from first call to final report.
