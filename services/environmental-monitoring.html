Environmental & Social Monitoring

Planned monitoring of environmental and social conditions on your sites, with clear reports you can show to regulators and lenders.

Who it is for

Operators and contractors with monitoring commitments in permits, management plans or loan conditions.

What is included

  • Monitoring plan with parameters, locations and frequency
  • Site visits and data review
  • Periodic monitoring reports
  • Grievance and community-engagement records review

What is not included

  • Laboratory analysis performed by accredited third-party labs, unless arranged for you
  • Monitoring services we have not confirmed we can provide on site

Method and steps

  1. Plan. Agree parameters, locations, methods and frequency.
  2. Monitor. Carry out visits, records and data checks.
  3. Report. Issue reports comparing results with limits and commitments.
  4. Follow up. Track corrective actions to closure.

Standards and laws we work to

  • Conditions in your permit and environmental management plan
  • Applicable Tanzanian environmental standards (confirm current versions)
  • IFC Performance Standards where relevant

Orientation only. Confirm current requirements with the regulator or a qualified adviser.

Frequently asked questions

What can be monitored?

Typical topics are dust, noise, water, waste and worker and community safety. The plan lists exactly what applies to your site.

How often will you report?

The frequency is set in the monitoring plan and follows your permit or lender requirements.

What you receive

  • Monitoring plan
  • Periodic monitoring reports
  • Corrective action tracker

Timeline and fees

Timeline and fee basis are set out in a written proposal after a short scoping conversation, because they depend on scope, site access and the standards required.
